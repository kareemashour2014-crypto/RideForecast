The map preview follows real streets when a road route can be computed; "Open route in Google Maps" always gives you the official, turn-by-turn driving route. A time buffer is added on top of driving time for typical West Bank checkpoint and traffic delays — actual fares and wait times may vary. Fare shown in ILS (₪). Suggestions blend live map data with a curated list of local streets and historical sites, and typos are matched to the closest known place. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
